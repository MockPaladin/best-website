The 2026 season: Byte to Bite

Farm-to-table food automation: design, build and program a robot from scratch using only the provided kit of parts. A BEST season runs from kickoff in the fall through a hub competition and, for top teams, a state championship.

We compete in the Hill Country BEST hub, and this year's hub competition is held at Dripping Springs High School.

  1. Sept 5, 2026

    Kickoff

    The "Byte to Bite " game was revealed and kits were distributed at 8:00 a.m. at NYOS Charter School. The eight-week build clock started.

  2. Sept - Oct

    Design & build

    Brainstorm, prototype, build and iterate on the robot and its practice field.

  3. Late Oct

    Driver training & notebook

    Driver training, notebook submission, and presentation rehearsal.

  4. Oct 31, 2026

    Practice Day

    Teams show off and test their robots at 8:00 a.m. at NYOS Charter School, ahead of hub game day.

  5. Nov 14, 2026

    Regional hub competition

    The Hill Country BEST hub competition is held at Dripping Springs High School. Game rounds, judging, and awards; top teams advance.

  6. Dec 9-12, 2026

    Texas BEST Championship

    Winning hub teams compete at Fair Park Coliseum and Briscoe Carpenter Livestock Center, Dallas, TX.
