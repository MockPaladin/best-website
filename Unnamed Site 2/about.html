About

Our team

BEST (Boosting Engineering, Science and Technology) is a nonprofit, volunteer-driven program that gives middle and high school students a taste of real engineering through a hands-on robotics competition.

Our team, Dripping Logistics (BEST Team 0456), at Dripping Springs High School takes on the annual game challenge from kickoff to competition. We are dedicated to creating an inclusive space where students of all backgrounds can engage in the exciting world of STEM through the BEST Robotics competition. We offer four teams to ensure everyone feels welcome: manufacturing to develop hands-on technical skills, programming to explore coding, the engineering notebook to document our design process, and business to promote the team's work through video design, t-shirt design, web design, posters and presentations. Whether you're new to robotics or an experienced enthusiast, we invite you to join us in building, learning, and growing together.

Faculty sponsor: Mr. Austin McCauley.

Teams

Manufacturing

CAD, prototyping, fabrication and testing the robot mechanisms.

Programming

Writing, testing and debugging the code that reads sensors, runs the motors and puts the robot under the driver 's control.

Engineering Notebook

Documenting the design process, decisions, and lessons learned.

Business

Team brand, social media, mascot, video design, t-shirt design, web design, posters and the marketing presentation.
